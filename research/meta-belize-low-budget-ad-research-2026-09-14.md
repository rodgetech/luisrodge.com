# Made by LR: low-budget Meta campaign for Belize

Research date: 14 September 2026

## Bottom line

Do **not** boost the existing post and do **not** start at BZ$25 per day.

The best first test is one Ads Manager campaign, one broad Belize ad set, one silent real-client Reel and one Messenger conversation path. Use a **US$21 lifetime budget over seven days** (about **BZ$42 total, or BZ$6 per day** at the official BZ$2 to US$1 peg). This is a diagnostic test, not a statistically conclusive experiment. Its job is to learn whether the combination of local proof, offer and Messenger follow-up can produce a genuinely qualified conversation without risking much money.

If the campaign produces no qualified conversation, stop at the cap and improve the offer or creative before spending again. If it produces at least two qualified conversations, or one booked consultation, extend the same setup with another small capped budget instead of immediately scaling.

## Why this structure fits Belize

Belize is a small national market. The Statistical Institute of Belize counted **397,483 people and 110,719 households** in the 2022 census. It also found home internet access in **71.3% of households** and a mobile phone in **90.9%**. Separately, 29.1% of employed people were self-employed in September 2024. These are not Meta audience-size estimates, but they establish that the reachable market is small, mobile-heavy and includes a meaningful owner-operator segment. Sources: [SIB census release](https://sib.org.bz/press-release_census-launch/), [SIB 2022 census key findings](https://sib.org.bz/wp-content/uploads/CensusKeyFindingsReport_2022.pdf), [SIB September 2024 labour force release](https://sib.org.bz/wp-content/uploads/LabourForce_2024-09.pdf).

**Inference for this campaign:** detailed interest and job-title targeting would divide an already small pool and may prevent Meta from finding enough people likely to start a conversation. Begin broad inside Belize, then let actual conversation quality—not assumptions about interests—tell us who responds.

The budget conversion uses the Central Bank of Belize's fixed exchange rate of **BZ$2 to US$1**. [Central Bank of Belize](https://www.centralbank.org.bz/detail-pages/news/2026/05/11/belize-commemorates-50-years-of-exchange-rate-stability)

## What current ads show

I reviewed the official Meta Ad Library for ads active in Belize on 14 September 2026. An exact search for **“web design Belize” returned no ads**. A broader **“website Belize” search returned about 15 ads**. This does not prove there are no competitors; the library only reflects currently active ads that match the search terms.

Useful local patterns included:

- **Belizing.com** (Library ID 1870115713394952) states one concrete benefit in one sentence: promotion on a Belize travel website, followed by a Learn More action.
- **Belize Automarket** (Library ID 2555254194898779) uses a real 36-second product/sourcing video, names a specific delivery promise and asks people to message.
- **Industrial Drone Works Ltd.** (Library ID 1527636291954102) leads with a costly business problem—avoiding flood-prone land and construction mistakes—and demonstrates the technical service with video.
- **FAH Golf Cart Rentals** (Library ID 1581894576763922) uses a 27-second real-service video, concrete benefits and a direct call action.
- Several other Belize ads were extremely long and contained competing calls to action. Their presence is evidence of what is running, not evidence that the format performs well.

The useful takeaway is not to imitate the loud or wordy ads. It is to combine their **specific local promise and direct response path** with Made by LR's strongest differentiator: polished proof of a real Belize business website. Review the live search in the [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=BZ&media_type=all&q=website%20Belize&search_type=keyword_unordered).

Meta's Ad Library does not expose reliable conversion, lead-quality or return-on-spend data for ordinary Belize commercial ads. We therefore cannot call any of these examples “winners” from the library alone. [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) is a transparency repository for currently running ads; the [Meta Ad Library API guide](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/api/) explains that richer spend and demographic fields apply to social-issue, election and political ads, while its expanded commercial-ad coverage is limited to specified regions.

## Recommended campaign

### Campaign setup

| Setting | Recommendation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Creation tool | Ads Manager, not Boost Post | We need explicit Messenger optimization, a conversation template, placement control and clean reporting. Meta's [Grow Your Business with Meta Ads](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/activity/673389) course explicitly covers when to use boosted posts versus Ads Manager. |
| Objective | Engagement | This is the current outcome objective used for a campaign whose desired action is a conversation rather than a video view or website visit. |
| Conversion location | Messaging apps | Keeps the action inside Meta and removes landing-page friction. |
| Destination | Messenger only | Matches the chosen customer contact channel and keeps the test interpretable. |
| Performance goal | Maximize messaging conversations started | Optimize for the action we value, not cheap reach, reactions or video views. |
| Buying/bid strategy | Auction, highest volume / lowest cost, no bid cap | A bid cap would add another unknown to a test with no account history. |
| Budget | US$21 lifetime | Hard, small total-risk boundary: about BZ$42. |
| Schedule | Seven complete days | Covers weekdays and a weekend without dividing the spend across multiple tests. |
| Campaign/ad-set/ad count | 1 / 1 / 1 | Prevents a tiny budget from being fragmented. |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements | Meta recommends Advantage+ placements to let delivery find efficient inventory across Facebook, Instagram, Messenger and Audience Network, including Reels. |
| Existing post | Do not use | The posted Reel has almost no useful social proof, while a fresh ad lets us use purpose-built copy, headline and Messenger flow. |

Meta's official Reels guidance recommends Advantage+ placements and says manual campaigns should otherwise use six or more placements. It also recommends native 9:16 video, safe-zone messaging and testing. [Meta Reels advertising guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)

Meta Blueprint treats ads that click to Messenger as a dedicated workflow covering objective, audience, budget, placements, creative, measurement and the post-click experience. [Meta Blueprint Business Messaging](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/page/541668-business-messaging) and [Connect with customers using Messenger](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253048-messenger-connect-with-customers)

### Audience

- Location: **people living in Belize**, nationwide.
- Age: **25–60**.
- Gender: all.
- Language: leave blank. English-only interface targeting can exclude Belizeans who use Facebook in another language.
- Detailed targeting: none in the first test.
- Audience automation: Advantage+ audience where available, with Belize and age used as the campaign controls rather than optional suggestions.
- Exclusions: only confirmed existing clients, if a clean first-party list already exists. Do not build speculative exclusions.

This is a campaign-specific inference from Belize's scale, not a claim that broad targeting always wins. Meta Blueprint's current audience curriculum specifically includes audience selection, detailed targeting, Custom Audiences and Advantage+ audience. [Meta Blueprint: Planning for your campaign audience](https://trainingworkshops.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253166-advantage-plus-audiences)

## The ideal ad

### Creative

Use the revised **18-second, 1080 × 1920 JetCarsBelize case-study Reel** as a new ad rather than boosting the organic post. It should remain fully understandable with sound off, because the creator has chosen not to use audio.

Proposed on-screen sequence:

1. **0–2 seconds:** “Your business deserves more than a social page.”
2. **2–6 seconds:** Real JetCars home page. “Built to be remembered.”
3. **6–10 seconds:** Packages and choices. “Clear enough to choose.”
4. **10–14 seconds:** Booking flow. “Easy to act.”
5. **14–18 seconds:** “Your website could be next.” Then: “Message WEBSITE for a 3 point review.”

Keep every essential line inside the Reels safe zone. Do not add a redundant Made by LR end label; the Page identity already supplies the advertiser name. Do not let Meta automatically add music or generative visual changes for this first controlled test.

Meta reports stronger average Reels performance from 9:16 creative with audio and safe-zone messaging, but that result does **not** mean audio is mandatory, nor was its study specific to Belizean web-design buyers. The honest compromise is a silent ad designed to be completely legible without sound, while retaining vertical motion and safe-zone text. [Meta Reels advertising guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)

Meta's creative curriculum likewise emphasizes high-quality, mobile-first creative adapted to placements. [Meta Blueprint Creative Essentials](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/516513-creative-essentials-for-your-ad-campaign)

### Primary text

> Still relying on Facebook to explain your business?
>
> This is the website I built for JetCarsBelize, from choosing a ride to starting a booking.
>
> If your business is ready for a website built around how customers actually choose and act, message **WEBSITE**. I’ll reply with a personal 3 point review of what makes sense for your business.

### Headline and button

- Headline: **Your website could be next.**
- Description: leave blank.
- Call to action: **Send Message**.

This copy makes one promise, proves it with a recognizable Belize project and sets a low-pressure expectation for the conversation. It avoids claiming guaranteed growth or inventing JetCars performance results.

## Messenger experience

The click is only useful if the conversation is easy and answered promptly. Meta Blueprint treats the post-click flow and Inbox management as part of the campaign, and Meta provides an Instant Reply automation in Business Suite. [Meta Blueprint Business Messaging](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/page/541668-business-messaging), [Meta Help: Messenger greeting and instant reply](https://www.facebook.com/help/1698046970464236/)

Recommended prefilled customer message:

> WEBSITE. I’d like the 3 point review for my business.

Recommended instant reply:

> Thanks for reaching out. Send your Facebook page or current website and tell me what you want customers to do: book, buy, call, visit or request a quote. I’ll take a look and reply personally.

Quick replies:

- I need a website
- I need an app
- I already have a website

Human follow-up, in this order:

1. “What does your business do?”
2. “What do you want the website or app to help customers do?”
3. “When would you like to start?”
4. Only after understanding the need: give the likely price range or offer a short text-based consultation.

Do not ask seven questions at once, demand a phone call or push audio. The goal is a comfortable text conversation that reveals whether the prospect has a real business, a real need and a workable timeline.

## Measurement and stop rules

### Primary outcome

A **qualified Messenger conversation**, defined before launch as a person who provides:

- the name or type of a real business;
- a real website/app need;
- a plausible timeline; and
- willingness to discuss the project's scope or budget.

### Reporting dashboard

Track:

- amount spent;
- reach, impressions and frequency;
- 3-second video plays, average watch time and completed plays as creative diagnostics;
- outbound/Messenger clicks;
- messaging conversations started and cost per conversation;
- qualified conversations, booked consultations and signed clients in a simple manual log.

Meta Ads Manager metrics describe delivery; the manual conversation log describes business quality. A cheap message is not success if it is spam or has no plausible project.

### Rules for the US$21 test

- Do a zero-cost end-to-end test of the Send Message button, quick replies and Page notifications before launch.
- Make no creative, audience or budget edits during the first 72 hours unless the ad or Messenger path is broken.
- Never spend beyond the US$21 lifetime cap in phase one.
- If the ad reaches roughly 500 impressions with no Messenger click, pause and rework the hook or offer. This is our operating threshold, not a Meta benchmark.
- If people click but do not send, shorten or clarify the welcome flow.
- If messages arrive but none qualify, revise the copy to state the service and expected project range more clearly before testing again.
- If the test yields at least two qualified conversations or one booked consultation, continue the same ad with another capped US$21 test. Do not create multiple audiences yet.

Meta warns that early delivery has a learning phase and provides reporting tools for making informed decisions; it does not promise that a particular small spend will generate a client. [Meta Blueprint: Getting started with your first ad](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/activity/707409) and [Meta Ads Manager learning path](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/collection/507792-meta-ads-manager-learning)

## What research cannot establish before spending

We cannot honestly know in advance:

- the cost per Belize Messenger conversation;
- how many conversations will be qualified;
- whether the audience will prefer “website” or “app” language;
- whether this creative will win against a future variant;
- whether one week will produce a client; or
- whether Meta's global Reels averages apply to this small, high-consideration Belize service.

The official Ads Library shows what is running, not which ordinary Belize ads are profitable. A US$21 test is also too small for a formal A/B test. Its value is bounded learning: it can reveal obvious delivery, attention, click and conversation problems while protecting cash. Meta's own testing guidance says to isolate variables and test, but valid inference requires adequate data. [Meta Reels advertising guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) and [Meta Blueprint measurement curriculum](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/collection/515315-meta-blueprint-startup-hub)

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Review the final silent Reel in the real ad preview for Feed, Stories and Reels safe zones.
- [ ] Confirm the Page, Messenger destination and Send Message button.
- [ ] Turn on and test the Messenger instant reply and quick replies.
- [ ] Use one campaign, one ad set and one ad.
- [ ] Confirm the lifetime budget is exactly US$21 and the end date is seven days later.
- [ ] Confirm Advantage+ placements and broad Belize audience.
- [ ] Save a reporting preset and a manual lead-quality log.
- [ ] Keep every object paused until final launch approval.
- [ ] After approval, launch once and do not edit for 72 hours unless broken.

No spend is justified until every checklist item passes and the final campaign preview is approved.
