# Belize web-design Facebook/Instagram ad research

**Prepared:** September 8, 2026

**Decision:** What first Meta ad gives Luis the best plausible chance of winning one more website client in Belize, using JetCarsBelize as the proof piece?

## Recommendation in one sentence

Run one proof-first, 15–25 second vertical video to Belize business owners in tourism and adjacent premium service categories, showing JetCarsBelize moving from cinematic first impression into its live availability and enquiry flow, and invite qualified owners to message **“WEBSITE”** on WhatsApp for a short, personal website opportunity review.

No source can establish in advance that an ad “will convert.” The defensible approach is to make one strong market hypothesis, define what a *qualified* lead means, and test it cheaply enough to learn without fragmenting Belize’s small audience.

## What the evidence actually establishes

### Channel and market

- **Facebook has meaningful local reach.** DataReportal, using Meta advertising-planning data from late 2025, reports potential Facebook ad reach of 238,000 people in Belize and the equivalent of 81.2% of Belizean adults. Instagram’s estimated reach was 144,000. These are **advertising reach estimates, not active-user counts**, and Meta revises them; they justify testing Meta, not assuming that every person is reachable or commercially relevant. [DataReportal: Digital 2026 Belize](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-belize)
- **Belize is digitally reachable beyond the large towns.** ITU reports that 80% of individuals in Belize used the internet in 2024, with 87.2% urban and 74.8% rural usage. That supports nationwide delivery rather than restricting the first test to Belize City alone. [ITU Belize Digital Development Dashboard](https://datahub.itu.int/data/?Affordability=ICT+prices&Connectivity=Infrastructure+development+and+sharing&Governance=Legal+frameworks&Markets=Spectrum&Sustainability=Emergency+telecommunications&Trust=Cybersecurity&e=BLZ)
- **Tourism is a commercially meaningful niche.** The Belize Tourism Board projected 551,698 overnight stays and 967,214 cruise passenger arrivals for 2025. [BTB, February 2, 2026](https://www.belizetourismboard.org/preliminiary-2025-tourism-figures-indicate-strong-forward-momentum/)
- **The potential owner/operator audience is real, but budget sensitivity is also real.** SIB’s April 2025 labour survey reports that tourism represented 13.5% of employment, wholesale/retail 16.9%, real estate/renting/business activities 9.1%, and 28.1% of employed people were self-employed. [SIB Labour Force Survey, April 2025](https://sib.org.bz/wp-content/uploads/LabourForce_2025-04.pdf)
- **Belize MSMEs explicitly want websites, marketing, payments and business systems, while citing cost, limited time/skills and unclear value as obstacles.** This comes from 153 applicants to BELTRAIDE’s DIBED digital-transformation program, not a representative census, so it is directional evidence. Applicants named increased sales, reach, engagement, visibility and trust as desired outcomes. [BELTRAIDE DIBED applicant report, October 2025](https://beltraide.bz/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/DIBED-Article-Report-Cohort-1-Template-FINAL.pdf)

### Creative and lead mechanism

- Meta recommends creative built natively for Reels and says 9:16 video with audio and key messages inside the safe zone produced a 34.5% lower cost per result than image ads in its cited set of 15 Reels-only split tests. That study spans several industries and countries, not Belize or web design, so it supports the **format**, not a Belize conversion forecast. Meta also recommends Advantage+ placements and testing. [Meta for Business: Reels ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads)
- Meta’s own lead-generation guide supports leads through Messenger, Instagram Direct and WhatsApp and reports that its tested “Instant forms + Messenger” setup increased reach and reduced average cost per lead versus instant forms alone. Again, this is platform-level evidence, not proof that the combination will yield qualified Belize web-design buyers. [Meta lead-generation guide](https://about.fb.com/ltam/wp-content/uploads/sites/14/2023/11/LeadGenerationGuide.pdf)
- Meta’s WhatsApp product page confirms that ads on Facebook and Instagram can open a WhatsApp conversation and support lead-generation objectives. [WhatsApp Business: ads that click to WhatsApp](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/)
- WhatsApp’s current help documentation says these ads can lead directly into a WhatsApp Business chat and that Ads Manager reports impressions, clicks and messaging conversations started. A WhatsApp Business account is required. [WhatsApp Help Center: create an ad that clicks to WhatsApp](https://faq.whatsapp.com/491963319630082)
- Meta announced Advantage+ leads in February 2025 and reported lower average cost per lead across its 20 cited tests, including professional services and B2B. The tests were not in Belize, and the qualified-lead result had weaker statistical confidence than the cost-per-lead result, so this supports trying the automation rather than treating it as guaranteed. [Meta announcement, February 6, 2025](https://about.fb.com/ltam/news/2025/02/maximiza-el-rendimiento-de-tu-campana-con-las-nuevas-funciones-de-advantage-ai-y-recomendaciones-probadas-empiricamente/)
- The [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) is useful for seeing current creative, wording, formats and run dates. For ordinary commercial ads in Belize it does **not** disclose conversions, qualified leads, cost or revenue, so an active ad cannot honestly be labelled “high converting” from the library alone. Meta’s API documentation says broader spend, impressions and demographic data are supplied for political/social-issue ads and certain EU/UK ads, not ordinary Belize service ads. [Meta Ad Library API](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/api/)

**Inference:** the visible competitive opening is not an unclaimed keyword; it is a more credible form of proof. The library cannot tell us whether any of those ads produced sales.

## Best buyer segments

### 1. First: tourism and experience operators

Tour operators, boat charters, fishing/diving operators, golf-cart and vehicle rentals, transfers, boutique accommodations, destination wedding venues and activity providers.

Why this is the strongest first hypothesis:

- JetCarsBelize is directly analogous proof.
- Their product is visual, so the creative communicates value quickly.
- Their customer often researches remotely and needs to understand, trust and act before arrival.
- Booking, availability, packages, guest questions and WhatsApp follow-up are concrete business problems Luis can visibly solve.

### 2. Adjacent premium visual businesses

Villas/resorts, real-estate and property-service firms, upscale restaurants, spas/wellness, construction/design firms and event businesses. The visual proof transfers well, but the booking interaction is less exact.

### 3. Later: established professional services and larger retail

Law, accounting, clinics, private schools and established retail can support higher-value projects, but JetCarsBelize is weaker category proof. Give them a separate creative later rather than diluting the first ad.

### Do not lead with “all small businesses in Belize”

That audience contains both high-value owners and highly price-sensitive microbusinesses. BELTRAIDE’s applicant evidence suggests many need the commercial value explained and face real cash/time constraints. The creative itself should qualify: **“Own a tour, rental, resort, restaurant or premium service?”**

## The ad to make

### Concept: “Built in Belize. Built to work.”

Do not make a static graphic with a laptop mockup and a list of services. Make the website the spectacle, then prove it has a job.

**Format:** 9:16 vertical video, 15–25 seconds, captions burned in, restrained sound design or music, all critical type inside Meta’s safe zone. Produce companion 4:5 and 1:1 crops from the same edit for feed placements.

**Storyboard**

| Time | Visual | On-screen words / voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | The strongest full-screen JetCarsBelize hero shot, immediately moving | **“This isn’t a Miami website.”** |
| 2–5s | Logo/URL plus a confident mobile scroll | **“It was designed and launched right here in Belize.”** |
| 5–10s | Experience/package selection and a date/time interaction | **“Not just beautiful. It helps customers understand, choose and enquire.”** |
| 10–15s | Quick desktop/mobile pairing; show a clean booking step | **“Brand. Mobile. Booking flow. One custom system.”** |
| 15–20s | Luis briefly on camera, or a restrained personal title card if he does not want to appear | **“I’m looking for the next Belize business ready to look world-class.”** |
| 20–25s | JetCarsBelize final frame + WhatsApp cue | **“Message WEBSITE for a free 10-minute opportunity review.”** |

The “Miami” hook is a recommended creative hypothesis: it counters the belief that premium digital work must come from abroad and makes the Belize proof surprising. A safer alternate is **“I built this for a Belize business. Yours could be next.”**

Use only site screens, brand marks, photos and client references Luis has permission to advertise. Do not claim that JetCarsBelize increased bookings, revenue or conversion rate until analytics establish that.

### Primary text

> **Belize business owners:** I just launched JetCarsBelize—a custom website designed around the actual business, not a recycled template.
>
> It makes the experience easy to understand, shows customers what is available, and gives them a clear path to enquire.
>
> If your resort, tour, rental, restaurant or premium service has outgrown a Facebook-only presence—or a website that no longer represents it—message me **WEBSITE**. I’ll send you a short, no-pressure opportunity review with three specific things I would improve.

**Headline:** `Could your business feel this good online?`

**Description:** `See the work. Get a personal 3-point review.`

**CTA:** `Send WhatsApp Message`

Only add scarcity such as “one project this month” if that is literally true and Luis will enforce it.

## Offer and lead flow

### Offer

The offer is not “a free consultation.” That sounds generic and asks the prospect to spend time before receiving value. Offer a **personal 3-point website opportunity review** delivered by voice note or a short screen recording. It should answer:

1. What is currently costing the business trust or enquiries?
2. What would make the offer easier for a customer to understand and act on?
3. What is the smallest sensible project: focused landing page, full marketing site, or booking/commerce system?

This demonstrates judgment without promising free design work. Keep it to ten minutes of Luis’s production time and only create it after basic qualification.

### WhatsApp qualification

Use the Leads objective with WhatsApp as the messaging destination if that option is available in Luis’s account. The prefilled opener can be:

> Hi Luis — WEBSITE. I’d like the 3-point review for my business.

Reply with four short prompts, preferably one at a time:

1. “What business do you run, and where in Belize are you based?”
2. “Send your website or Facebook/Instagram page.”
3. “What should the website help a customer do: book, buy, request a quote, visit, or something else?”
4. “Are you hoping to start now, in the next 1–3 months, or later?”

After the review, invite suitable prospects to a 15-minute discovery call. A **qualified conversation** is a Belize owner/decision-maker with a real business, a concrete customer action, and plausible intent to begin within three months—not simply anyone who says “price?”

If WhatsApp leads are unavailable or operationally awkward, the fallback is an Instant Form with the same four questions and a clear permission checkbox for follow-up. Do not use a one-tap name/phone form with no qualifying question; the business outcome is qualified conversations, not cheap form fills.

## Small-budget test

This is a recommendation, not a Meta or Belize benchmark.

| Setting | First test |
| --- | --- |
| Objective | Leads → Messaging → WhatsApp |
| Geography | Belize, nationwide |
| Age | 25–60 to start |
| Language | English; do not restrict the account-language field unless delivery data shows a need |
| Audience | Broad/Advantage+ audience; let the creative qualify the niche |
| Placements | Advantage+ placements, with proper 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 assets |
| Structure | One campaign, one ad set, one hero ad |
| Budget | **BZ$25/day for 14 days = BZ$350 total** (about US$175) |
| Optimization | Messaging conversations/leads, not traffic or video views |

Why keep it simple: Belize is small. Several towns, interests and ad sets would divide a modest budget into samples too thin to interpret. One broad audience also avoids assuming Meta can accurately identify every local “business owner.”

For a more informative test, add **one** alternate edit in the same ad set after the hero ad is ready: identical body and CTA, but use the safer hook (“I built this for a Belize business”) versus the “Miami” hook. If BZ$350 is the hard ceiling, however, one excellent ad is preferable to four mediocre variants.

### Review at days 7 and 14

Track these in a simple sheet:

- Spend
- Messaging conversations started
- Qualified conversations
- Reviews actually delivered
- Discovery calls booked and attended
- Proposals sent
- Client won and project value

Do not select the winner by clicks, video views or raw cost per message. Calculate:

`cost per qualified conversation = spend / qualified conversations`

`cost per attended discovery call = spend / attended calls`

At day 7, fix only obvious delivery, destination or tracking problems. At day 14:

- **Few conversations:** the hook/creative or audience is not earning attention; test a new opening, not a longer paragraph.
- **Messages but few qualified owners:** tighten the sector callout and the offer/qualification.
- **Qualified owners but no calls:** improve the review and next-step invitation; inspect follow-up speed.
- **Calls but no proposals:** the mismatch is discovery, scope or buying readiness, not the ad.
- **Proposals but no sale:** inspect pricing, trust, proposal clarity and fit before spending more.

With a small sample, do not invent a universal CPL target. The economic test is whether one realistic client value comfortably exceeds ad spend plus Luis’s review/sales time.

## What to avoid

- A generic laptop mockup with “Need a website?”
- Leading with a discount or the cheapest package
- A long services list in the first three seconds
- Targeting every industry in one piece of copy
- Sending cold traffic to a general portfolio homepage with no singular next step
- Claiming bookings, sales, speed, SEO rankings or conversion gains that are not measured
- Calling an Ad Library example “high-performing” when its performance data is unavailable
- Making a free homepage mockup for every unqualified lead

## Bottom line

JetCarsBelize should not appear as one thumbnail in a portfolio collage. It is the ad. The first campaign should sell the difference between “being online” and having a custom customer journey: premium Belize-specific presentation, mobile clarity and a direct path to action. Tourism is the right first niche because the proof is exact; WhatsApp is the right first conversation path because it shortens the distance between admiration and qualification. The proposed BZ$350 test will not prove a universal formula, but it is enough to learn whether this proof-and-review offer can produce the one outcome that matters: a serious Belize business owner entering a sales conversation.

### What the local market is teaching us

The recurring category language is “more traffic,” “more sales,” “affordable,” “get found,” long feature inventories and generic consultation prompts. WhatsApp is already a familiar response path, so the campaign should keep it. The creative, however, should break the visual pattern:

1. Open with the real JetCarsBelize site moving full-screen—not a laptop mockup or branded flyer.
2. Let the viewer see one complete customer action: discover a ride, understand the offer, choose a date and reach the booking step.
3. Add Luis's face or voice briefly so the premium work has a local human author.
4. Make one precise offer: a personal three-point website opportunity review, delivered by voice note or short screen recording.
5. Avoid unmeasured claims about increased bookings or revenue. The demonstrated experience is the proof.

### Revised recommended opening

The competitor review makes this opening stronger than the earlier geography-only hook:

> **Most web-design ads tell you what they can build. I'd rather show you.**

Then immediately show JetCarsBelize and say:

> **I designed and launched this for a San Pedro business. Now I'm looking for the next Belize business ready to look—and work—this good online.**

This is direct category contrast without attacking another agency. A useful second variant is the shorter local-pride line: **“Built in Belize, for a Belize business.”** Test only the opening; keep the same body, proof sequence, offer and WhatsApp CTA so the result is interpretable.
