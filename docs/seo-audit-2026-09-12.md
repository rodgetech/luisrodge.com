## Primary guidance

- [Google SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): useful content and links; changes can take hours to months, with no first-place guarantee.
- [Google title links](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link): clear, descriptive titles; displayed titles may differ.
- [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap): canonical URLs and accurate dates; no indexing guarantee.
- [Google Business Profile eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036): in-person contact requirements and online-only exclusion.
